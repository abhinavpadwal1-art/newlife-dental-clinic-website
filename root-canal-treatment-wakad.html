Root Canal Treatment in Wakad: Symptoms, Procedure & What to Expect

Root canal treatment is used to treat a tooth when the soft tissue inside it, called the dental pulp, becomes inflamed or infected. The need for treatment can only be confirmed after a dental examination and, where appropriate, diagnostic imaging.

When might a root canal be considered?

Symptoms that may lead someone to seek a dental assessment include persistent toothache, sensitivity to hot or cold, discomfort while biting, swelling, or changes around the affected tooth. These symptoms can have different causes, so they do not automatically mean that root canal treatment is required.

What happens during treatment?

If root canal treatment is appropriate, the affected tooth is accessed so the infected or inflamed tissue can be removed. The internal spaces are cleaned, shaped and sealed. Depending on the condition of the tooth, further restoration such as a crown may be discussed to help protect it.

Does root canal treatment hurt?

Local anaesthesia is commonly used during treatment. Individual experiences and recovery vary, and your dentist can explain what to expect based on the condition of your tooth.

When should you see a dentist?

Persistent dental pain, swelling or a damaged tooth should be assessed rather than self-diagnosed. Early evaluation can help identify the cause and appropriate treatment options.
