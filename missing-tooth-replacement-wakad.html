Missing Tooth Replacement Options in Wakad: What Can You Consider?

A missing tooth can affect appearance, chewing and the way the teeth function together. There are different replacement approaches, and the right choice depends on the location of the gap, condition of surrounding teeth, oral health and individual preferences.

Dental implants

An implant may be considered to support a replacement tooth without relying on neighbouring teeth for support. Suitability depends on several clinical and health factors and requires personalised assessment.

Dental bridges

A bridge is another fixed option used in suitable situations to replace a missing tooth. The design and suitability depend on the teeth around the space and the overall treatment plan.

Removable options

Removable tooth-replacement options may also be appropriate for some patients, particularly when several teeth are missing. The advantages and limitations should be discussed during consultation.

How do you choose?

There is no single replacement option that is right for every patient. A dental examination allows the available choices to be compared based on your individual clinical situation.
