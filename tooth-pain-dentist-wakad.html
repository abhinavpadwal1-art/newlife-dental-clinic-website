Tooth Pain in Wakad: When Should You Visit a Dentist?

Tooth pain can have several causes, and the intensity of pain alone does not identify the underlying problem. A dental examination is the appropriate way to determine why a tooth hurts and what treatment, if any, is needed.

What can cause tooth pain?

Possible causes include tooth decay, inflammation or infection inside a tooth, gum problems, a cracked or damaged tooth, sensitivity and problems around a wisdom tooth. Because different conditions can produce similar symptoms, self-diagnosis can be misleading.

When should pain be assessed?

Persistent or worsening toothache, pain when biting, prolonged sensitivity, swelling or discomfort associated with a damaged tooth are reasons to arrange a dental assessment. Significant swelling or symptoms affecting general health require prompt professional attention.

Does toothache always mean root canal treatment?

No. Root canal treatment is appropriate only for particular dental conditions. Other causes of pain may require different management. Examination and diagnostic tests help the dentist identify the source of symptoms.
