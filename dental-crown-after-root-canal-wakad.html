Restorative dentistry

Do You Need a Dental Crown After Root Canal Treatment?

After root canal treatment, the tooth still needs an appropriate restoration. In some situations a dental crown may be recommended to protect and restore the tooth, but the correct restoration depends on the individual tooth and the amount of remaining tooth structure.

Why might a crown be considered?

Teeth that have required root canal treatment may already have been weakened by decay, previous restorations or structural damage. A crown can be considered when greater coverage and protection are clinically appropriate.

Does every root canal tooth need a crown?

Not necessarily. Tooth location, remaining structure, bite forces and other clinical factors influence the restorative plan. Your dentist should assess the tooth before recommending the most appropriate restoration.

Why is follow-up important?

Completing the restorative phase is an important part of the overall treatment plan. Your dentist can explain the recommended timing, care instructions and review needs for the treated tooth.
