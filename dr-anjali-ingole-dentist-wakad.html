A clear, patient-first approach

Dental concerns can range from routine preventive needs to tooth pain, damaged or missing teeth and questions about appearance or alignment. At Newlife Dental Clinic, the emphasis is on clinical assessment and treatment planning based on each patient’s individual oral-health needs rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Dental care at Newlife Dental Clinic

The clinic provides general and restorative dental care and consultations for treatments including professional dental cleaning, root canal treatment, crowns and bridges, dental implants, teeth whitening, braces and clear aligners. The suitability of any procedure depends on clinical assessment.

Serving Wakad and Hinjawadi

Newlife Dental Clinic is located in the Wakad–Hinjawadi area and is convenient for patients from nearby neighbourhoods including Tathawade, Thergaon, Punawale and Pimple Saudagar.

When should you book a dental consultation?

You may consider a dental assessment if you have persistent tooth pain, sensitivity, swelling, bleeding gums, a damaged or missing tooth, or simply need a preventive check-up. An examination helps identify the cause and allows appropriate options to be discussed.
