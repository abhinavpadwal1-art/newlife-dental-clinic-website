Wisdom Tooth Pain in Wakad: When Is Dental Evaluation Needed?

Wisdom teeth are the last teeth at the back of the mouth. Some erupt without causing significant problems, while others may be partially erupted, difficult to clean or positioned in a way that requires assessment.

Why can a wisdom tooth hurt?

Discomfort around a wisdom tooth can have different causes, including inflammation of the surrounding tissues, decay, pressure or other local dental problems. An examination is needed to identify the reason for symptoms.

What symptoms warrant assessment?

Persistent pain, swelling, difficulty cleaning the area, repeated inflammation or discomfort while chewing are reasons to discuss the area with a dentist. More significant swelling or symptoms affecting general wellbeing warrant prompt professional attention.

Does every wisdom tooth need removal?

No. Whether treatment is needed depends on the tooth’s position, symptoms, oral health and clinical findings. If removal is being considered, the dentist can explain why and discuss the appropriate next steps.
