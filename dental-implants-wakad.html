Dental Implants in Wakad: What to Know Before Replacing a Missing Tooth

A dental implant is one option that may be considered when replacing a missing tooth. Whether it is suitable depends on individual dental and medical factors, so treatment planning begins with a clinical assessment.

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is placed in the jaw to support a replacement tooth. The complete treatment plan can involve several stages, and the exact approach varies according to the condition of the mouth and the tooth or teeth being replaced.

Who may be suitable for implants?

Factors considered during assessment can include the health of the teeth and gums, available bone, the location of the missing tooth, medical history and individual treatment requirements. Not every patient or site is automatically suitable.

What happens before treatment?

Your dentist will examine the area and discuss your goals. Diagnostic imaging may be recommended where clinically appropriate. Alternative tooth-replacement options can also be discussed so you understand the choices available.

Why is personalised planning important?

Implant treatment is not a one-size-fits-all procedure. A personalised assessment helps determine whether an implant is appropriate and what treatment stages may be needed.
