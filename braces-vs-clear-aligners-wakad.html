Braces vs Clear Aligners: Which Teeth-Straightening Option May Be Right for You?

Braces and clear aligners are both used to move teeth, but the most appropriate option depends on your teeth, bite, treatment goals and the complexity of the movements required.

How do braces work?

Braces use brackets and wires to apply controlled forces to the teeth. Different types of orthodontic problems may be treated with braces depending on clinical assessment and treatment planning.

How do clear aligners work?

Clear aligners use a planned sequence of removable trays to gradually move teeth. Because they are removable, following the recommended wear schedule is an important part of treatment.

Which option is better?

Neither option is universally better. Some patients may value the removability and appearance of aligners, while braces may be more appropriate for certain tooth movements or treatment requirements. Suitability should be determined after an orthodontic assessment.

What should you consider?

Your bite, tooth position, oral health, lifestyle, treatment expectations and ability to follow the treatment plan can all influence the choice. A consultation allows these factors to be evaluated before deciding.
